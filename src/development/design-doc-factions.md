Faction Gameplay
=====================
To the end of trying to create an immersive environment, it would be desirable to see an increased use of factions to help define the setting. This should not necessarily include extensive lore, but increased use of factions within gameplay that have clear goals, backgrounds, and themes that players can use to shape their characters and immerse themselves in the setting. 

## Changes
### The Factions in Question
The primary factions would consist of Nanotrasen, The Syndicate, The USSP, and SolGov as well as various minor factions. Minor factions can be created to suit certain niches, but should still have the same requirements and level of theming as major factions. In the case of the Syndicate, each member organization and their specific goals/interests should be used where possible.

### Antags/Special Roles
Antags should be associated with an established faction wherever possible, and their playstyle should be influenced by the factions beliefs and goals. Additionally, there can be similar antags across different factions which play in different ways (for example, a traitor hired by Gorlex may behave differently and have different goals than a traitor from Interdyne, or the USSP). While there are advantages to leaving things open-ended, it is generally better to provide players with a prompt to work with; Providing a prompt gives players a baseline of how to act outside of just doing the goals they are given.
